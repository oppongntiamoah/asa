Frequently asked questions

Are the prices live?

No. GSE publishes closing prices once a day — every price in SikaTrack is labeled with the date it's from.

Is this investment advice?

No. SikaTrack is an informational portfolio tracker, not a licensed investment adviser or broker under Ghanaian securities law.

Which brokers can I upload statements from?

IC Securities and Black Star Advisors today. More brokers are being added over time — you can always add transactions manually in the meantime.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Danger zone → Request account deletion.
